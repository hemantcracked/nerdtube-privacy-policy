Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 11, 2026

Welcome to NerdTube ("we," "our," or "us"). Your privacy is deeply important to us. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding information collection, use, and disclosure when you use our application (the "Service").

1. Information Collection and Use

NerdTube does not collect, track, store, or share any personal data.

  • No Account Creation: You are not required to create an account or provide any personal information (such as your name, email address, or phone number) to use the Service.
  • No Server Storage: We do not maintain external servers to collect your usage data or personal identifiers.

2. Local Device Storage

All of your personal application data is stored strictly locally on your own device. This includes your:

  • Watch history
  • Bookmarks and saved items
  • Custom user settings and preferences

This data never leaves your device and is not accessible by us. If you delete the application or clear your device storage, this data will be permanently erased.

3. Third-Party Services (YouTube)

Our Service utilizes embedded video playback provided by YouTube.

  • When you play videos within NerdTube, you are interacting directly with YouTube's infrastructure.
  • YouTube may collect data such as your IP address, device information, cookies, and video playback statistics according to its own policies.
  • We do not control and are not responsible for how YouTube collects or uses your data. For more information, please review the Google Privacy Policy.

4. Children's Privacy

Because our Service does not collect any personal data from any user, it does not knowingly or intentionally collect personal information from children under the age of 13.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected by posting the new Privacy Policy directly on this page or within the application. You are advised to review this policy periodically for any changes.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us via our official project repository or contact channel.
